About

International affairs research, reporting and multimedia work grounded in documents, interviews and close attention to how public decisions affect people.

✎

I am an international affairs researcher and multimedia journalist. My work spans reported articles, policy analysis, video and digital projects, with a particular interest in the point where institutions and policy become ordinary experience.

Recent projects have examined trade and household prices in Uruguay, the Beirut port explosion, press freedom in the United States, environmental questions and cultural institutions. I analyze legislation, court records, government and multilateral reports, datasets, academic research and archival material.

I work in English, Spanish and French. Across formats, I make the reporting trail visible: what I read, who I spoke with, what remains uncertain and when the work was last checked.
